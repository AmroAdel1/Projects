Mediterranean Grilled Salmon

A perfectly grilled salmon fillet served with a colorful medley of roasted Mediterranean vegetables, finished with fresh herbs and a zesty lemon dressing. This dish is packed with omega-3 fatty acids and essential nutrients.

Chef Maria Rodriguez

Prep Time

15 min

Cook Time

15 min

Servings

4

Difficulty

Medium

Ingredients

4 salmon fillets (6 oz each)
2 red bell peppers, sliced
2 yellow bell peppers, sliced
2 zucchini, sliced
1 cup cherry tomatoes
4 cloves garlic, minced
3 tbsp olive oil
2 lemons (juice and zest)
Fresh herbs (parsley, dill, basil)
Salt and black pepper to taste

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Prepare the vegetables

Preheat oven to 425°F. Slice bell peppers and zucchini. Toss with 2 tbsp olive oil, garlic, salt, and pepper. Spread on a baking sheet.

2

Roast the vegetables

Roast vegetables in preheated oven for 20-25 minutes, stirring halfway through, until tender and lightly caramelized.

3

Season the salmon

Pat salmon fillets dry. Brush with remaining olive oil and season generously with salt, pepper, and lemon zest.

4

Grill the salmon

Heat grill to medium-high. Grill salmon skin-side down for 4-5 minutes, then flip and cook for another 3-4 minutes until desired doneness.

5

Plate and garnish

Arrange roasted vegetables on plates. Top with grilled salmon. Drizzle with lemon juice and garnish with fresh herbs. Serve immediately.

Pro Tips

  • For best results, use wild-caught salmon for higher omega-3 content
  • Don't overcook the salmon - it should be slightly translucent in the center
  • You can substitute vegetables based on seasonal availability
  • Leftovers can be stored in the fridge for up to 2 days
